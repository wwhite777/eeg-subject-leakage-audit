Workflow of the audit. (1) Two public datasets: PhysioNet motor movement/imagery (MMI), accessed through the EEG motor movement/imagery brain-computer interface (EEGBCI) dataset, and Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) Competition IV, dataset 2a (BCI-IV-2a, abbreviated IV-2a in the diagram). EEGBCI includes 109 subjects, 64 channels, four two-class paradigms and three runs per paradigm; IV-2a includes nine subjects, 22 channels and two sessions of six runs. EEG means electroencephalography; ch means recording channels; Hz means hertz. MI = motor imagery; ME = motor execution; L/R = left versus right. (2) Preprocessing uses zero-phase finite impulse response (FIR) band-pass filtering and cue-locked 0–4 s epochs; s denotes seconds. There is no rejection for artefacts; only cues whose full 4-s epoch lies within the recording are epoched. Networks receive per-trial z-scored trials, with a global-scale matched-preprocessing control. (3) Decoders are common spatial patterns with linear discriminant analysis (CSP+LDA), tangent space with logistic regression (TS+LR), ShallowFBCSPNet (shallow filter-bank common spatial pattern network) and EEGNet (electroencephalography network). Trainable-parameter counts are reported in the decoder-settings table. OAS = oracle-approximating shrinkage. The tangent reference is the training Fréchet mean; L2 denotes the squared Euclidean coefficient penalty. (4) The protocol ladder includes within-subject leave-one-run-out or session-to-session evaluation; P0, trial-random; P0s, subject-by-class-stratified trial-random; P1, run-disjoint but subject-overlapping; P1s, session-disjoint but subject-overlapping on IV-2a; and P2, subject-disjoint: subjects permuted and cut into three groups on EEGBCI; LOSO = leave-one-subject-out, the P2 protocol on IV-2a. On EEGBCI the outer training fractions are matched at two thirds. On IV-2a the trial-random protocols are P0a (six folds) and P0b (two folds), with subject-by-class-stratified counterparts P0a-s and P0b-s; its ladder training fractions differ. Partitions and network training are repeated over prespecified seeds. The protocols specify which recording unit may occur on both sides of the train/test boundary. Ladder differences are protocol contrasts. (5) The fixed-test framework partitions subjects into target (T), donor (D) and background (B) blocks, rotated across evaluations. For EEGBCI the test set is half of the last run of T, selected by alternating trials within subject and class. B in the arm labels denotes the shared background training trials from its other runs. Arm A0 combines B with count-matched D trials from other runs; A1 combines B with count-matched T trials from other runs; A2 combines B with T trials, replacing other-run trials with the unused half of the test run. Target and donor subjects are paired by trial count, with matching within run and class. Any count shortfall in the replacement is recorded. No test trial enters any training arm. For TS+LR, one tangent reference is fitted to B and shared across arms; the arm-specific reference variant is secondary. Networks share one class-stratified validation slice selected from B and removed from every optimization set, with paired training seeds. CSP filters and classifiers are fitted separately for each arm. A1 minus A0 is the target-inclusion contrast, conditional on the background and donor sampling; A2 minus A1 is the same-run-adjacency contrast. The IV-2a analogue uses half of the last session as test, first-session background and donor trials, and unused last-session trials in A2, giving a same-session contrast. (6) Controlled analyses include an inverse-penalty sweep (C is the inverse regularization strength; larger C means weaker penalty), principal component analysis (PCA) dimension sweeps, subject identity decoding, variance decomposition, subject-mean removal and Riemannian recentering, weight alignment, target-subject in/out reference tests, network-width sweeps, a subject-count (N) by feature-dimension (d) factorial, and a calibration-size curve. (7) Outputs are per-subject accuracies and paired protocol and matched-arm contrasts. CI = confidence interval; subject-bootstrap 95 % intervals accompany paired Wilcoxon and sign-flip tests with table-wise Holm correction. The IV-2a intervals are descriptive and unadjusted. TOST = two one-sided tests for equivalence against the prespecified margin of ±0.01 in the reference-mean analyses; TOST is not used for the new matched contrasts. The diagram shows the design, with no numerical results.
