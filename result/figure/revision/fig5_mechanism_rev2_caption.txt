(a) Order A (subject → class → interaction) sequential fractions of total tangent-feature sum of squares: subject = RSS0−RSS_S; class given subject = RSS_S−RSS_SC; subject × class given both = RSS_SC−RSS_cell; residual = RSS_cell; each divided by RSS0, summed over dimensions. Fits are ordinary trial-weighted least squares with intercepts, in a common tangent space (reference = Fréchet mean of all trials; descriptive). These sequential projection increments are not uniquely separated variance sources. (b) Multinomial logistic identity-probe accuracy (C = 1): (i) no centring, (ii) independent-reference centring, (iii) training-run centring, (v) split-reference centring with alternating reference-run halves, and (iv) all-trial centring, the revision-1 construction applied here across all three runs. Each of the six ordered (reference, training, test) run assignments uses a tangent reference fitted only on training-run covariances; reference-run features therefore depend on the training covariances through this mean. Bars average accuracy over assignments within subject, then over subjects; whiskers show the min–max of per-assignment macro accuracies, not confidence intervals. Subject-bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples; seed 20260916), pooled-trial accuracy and paired (ii)−(v) are given below. The difference (ii)−(v) estimates the identity signal that a shared estimated centring vector introduces. Dashed = 1/number of subjects. The rev1 P0/P1 bars retain the original all-trial-centred trial-random/run-disjoint 3-fold pooled accuracies; their raw accuracies are retained below. In the revision-1 two-subset probe, n_train × centred training mean + n_test × centred test mean = 0, forcing opposite residual centroids and potentially below-chance test accuracy. Scheme (iv) here instead obeys a three-run weighted-zero relation including the reference run. Linear-probe performance under these assignments does not establish removal of identity information, biological identity, motor-label exploitation or a mediation fraction.
